# Code of conduct

Contributors must keep technical discussion specific, evidence-based, and directed at the work rather than the person.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, sexual harassment, and deliberate disruption are not accepted in project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

Good-faith disagreement, direct technical criticism, benchmark challenges, and rejection of weak proposals are allowed. Civility does not require pretending that every design is equally sound.

Report conduct problems privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub contact channel. Enforcement decisions should document the behavior, relevant context, and action taken while limiting unnecessary disclosure.
